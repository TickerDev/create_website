About me

I’m Dor Elboim, a Computer Science student at Texas Tech University and a builder working across AI, business software, and gaming. I enjoy taking an idea from the first prototype to a product people can use.

I co-founded Tebstack, an AI-powered support solution for gaming storefronts. It automates customer support and adapts to the needs of each store. My interest in B2B software comes from this kind of problem: helping businesses spend less time on repetitive work and more time serving their customers.

Gaming is a big part of my work. Through Ticker Scripts, I create and sell digital assets for the FiveM platform that are used by hundreds of players. I also founded and ran Niro Team, where I designed, programmed, and shipped architecture that served more than 1.5 million unique users.

I’ve explored generative AI through DXR Labs and led a designer and web developer at Rylo Group on gaming-related web platforms. These projects have given me experience in programming, product development, and team leadership. I’m interested in connecting with people building useful AI tools, business software, and gaming products.
